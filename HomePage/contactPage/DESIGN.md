## Brand & Style
The design system is rooted in the "Premium Farm-to-Fork" aesthetic, blending the rugged authenticity of Kerala’s spice plantations with the sophisticated precision of a high-end global exporter. It targets bulk buyers and culinary professionals who demand both heritage and modern reliability. 

The visual style is **Sophisticated Organic**. It utilizes high-contrast layouts where deep, earthy tones meet clean, airy backgrounds. The experience should feel immersive, evoking the sensory richness of raw spices through layered textures, while maintaining a professional, corporate structure that builds international trust.

## Colors
The palette is a tribute to the spice origin. **Deep Moss Green** (#607D2B) serves as the primary brand anchor, representing lush vegetation and organic growth. **Saffron Gold** (#F59E0B) is used as a strategic accent for calls-to-action and quality indicators, mimicking the vibrant hue of ground turmeric and saffron.

The background uses a **Cream/Off-white** base (#FCF9F2) rather than pure white to soften the digital experience and provide a "parchment" or "organic paper" feel. **Slate Black** (#171717) is reserved for high-contrast typography and structural elements, ensuring readability and a premium, grounded weight to the interface.

## Typography
This design system employs a sophisticated typographic pairing to balance tradition and modernity. **EB Garamond** is the headline face; its classical proportions and elegant serifs communicate heritage, artisanal quality, and the "Premium" nature of the brand.

**Hanken Grotesk** provides a clean, contemporary counterpoint for body text and functional UI elements. It ensures high legibility for technical specifications and export data. Use generous line heights for body copy to maintain the "airy" luxury feel. Labels and small navigation items should use Hanken Grotesk in uppercase with slight tracking to reinforce a professional, systematic tone.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy on desktop to maintain a curated, editorial feel. A 12-column grid is used with a generous 1280px max-width, allowing for wide margins that provide the "luxury of space."

On mobile, the layout transitions to a single-column fluid flow with 20px side margins. Spacing rhythm is strictly 8px-based (the "base" unit). Use large vertical padding (80px–120px) between sections on the homepage to allow the high-quality spice photography and textures to breathe, preventing the "cluttered" look common in commodity trading platforms.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layers** and **Subtle Texturing** rather than heavy drop shadows. Surfaces use the "Cream Paper" base, with secondary containers using a slightly darker or more "toasted" off-white to define hierarchy.

To reinforce the organic theme, use very soft, diffused shadows (0%–5% opacity) with a slight Moss Green tint to ground cards. Immersive sections may use backdrop blurs over high-resolution imagery of spices or landscapes, but interactive elements should remain flat and crisp to emphasize reliability.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Soft (Level 1)**. Elements feature subtle 4px corner radii which provide a touch of approachability without losing the structured, professional feel required for a B2B export brand. 

Containers for images of raw ingredients may occasionally use organic, slightly asymmetrical "blob" masks or circular frames to contrast with the rigid rectangularity of the "Modern Reliability" side of the brand.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Primary buttons use the Saffron Gold with Slate Black text for maximum visibility. Secondary buttons use a Deep Moss Green outline. All buttons use 4px rounding and uppercase labels.
- **Product Cards:** These should be "Elegant Product Cards" with a full-bleed spice texture at the top, a Cream background for the details, and the product name in EB Garamond. 
- **Bulk Inquiry Forms:** These are high-trust components. Use clean, outlined input fields with Hanken Grotesk labels. The submit button should be prominent and suggest a direct connection to the export team.
- **Certification Badges:** Use a specialized "Gold Medal" style. These should be displayed in a horizontal row with a subtle tint of the primary green to highlight organic/ISO/Fair Trade status.
- **Data Tables:** For spice specifications (oil content, moisture), use high-contrast, minimalist rows with Slate Black headers and Cream-tinted alternating row fills.
